Three of the four Universal Cycle stages had been opened over time. The gap was not unnoticed so much as misfiled: the second-round ADAPT option already says to widen the read scope, and the guideline ladder's repair duty already says to grep the touched files for the defect's pattern. Both are retrieval strategies, and both sit in a recovery path rather than in the stage they belong to. The prompt to open it came from a retrieval paper whose ablation ranks multi-round traversal above structured compilation and both above error repair — the order matters here, because the effort in this project has gone to the second of those and none to the first.

What went in is a ladder ordered by declaration status rather than cost: the anchor a role prompt or layer guide names, then the index, then the edges the build already computed, then content search for relations no edge declares, then the whole corpus. A rung counts by whether the ground was declared, not by what kind of operation reaches it, so a qmd search the layer guide prescribes as part of its own procedure is still the anchor rung. Rising happens only on a named insufficiency signal and goes straight to the rung the signal points at; sequential exhaustion was never the intent. The caps are provisional and say so — three empty results at a rung, fifteen expansion operations outside the anchor — because no measurement exists yet to set them, and the paper's values belong to the paper's corpus.

The instrumentation is one optional field. The author states its final rung at hand-off, the Editor-in-Chief carries it into the defect load, and it is never backfilled when a later ADAPT climbs higher, since stopping before that climb is the thing being measured. Without that axis every under-read defect arrives looking like an authoring defect, and the only prescription the corpus can ever produce is to fix the guide. Guideline editing was deliberately left off the ladder: at roughly 565 KB of Markdown, built from explicit links and a controlled vocabulary, the existing duty to re-read each touched file whole and grep the pattern already approximates exhaustion, and a ladder needs a cost gradient to justify itself.

The reachable ceiling is what makes this corpus different from the one upstream. There the exhaustive rung is out of reach by a wide margin and the ladder is pure necessity; here the whole wiki is about twenty thousand tokens, so reading everything remains an option. That is worth keeping rather than treating as a technicality, because it is the only place the ladder itself can be checked — whether the edge and search rungs surface what an exhaustive read surfaces is a question with an answer here and no answer where the exhaustive read cannot be run. The design argument stays in this entry; the body carries only the permission condition it licenses.
